Roster Report

The NCMB Roster is a full listing of the physicians, physician assistants, perfusionists, and residents regulated by the NCMB. It provides names, address, license numbers, area of practice, and more, but not phone numbers or email addresses.

A new roster report is run monthly to provide the most up-to-date listing of the Board's licensees. The Roster is provided in Excel file format and is available electronically.
