Program Director Welcome Message

Welcome to the MUSC Department of Anesthesia and Preoperative Medicine!

George Guldan III, M.D.
Associate Dean for GME, College of Medicine
Joanne M. Conroy, M.D. Endowed Chair for Education & Leadership Development
Residency Program Director

Congratulations on selecting such a challenging and rewarding specialty in medicine. We are excited you are considering MUSC for your residency training. Our anesthesia residency offers an integrated 4-year training program (intern year and 3-year residency) that produces excellent anesthesiologists with extensive experience in every area of the field.

For more information on our program, please explore our 25-26 MUSC Anesthesia Residency Brochure.

Click here to watch a short video from our Program Director and Residents!
